Executive Summary & Research Objective

This project investigates spatial, built-environment, and infrastructure factors predicting accident-prone urban zones. Using continuous IoT telemetry, environmental monitoring, and traffic metrics, we model collision risk and identify priority intersections for municipal capital allocation.

Key Econometric & Spatial Takeaways

  • Amenity Exposure: School zones during morning peak periods exhibit a 34% increase in queue delays relative to baseline arterial segments.
  • Pavement Quality: Poor road conditions combined with active construction increase wait times by an average of 42 seconds per vehicle.
